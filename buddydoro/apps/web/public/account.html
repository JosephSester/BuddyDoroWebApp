Help & FAQ

Common actions and answers

How do I earn Doros?

Complete focus sessions using the timer. You earn 1 Doro for every focused minute — the longer your session, the more you earn.

How do I change my Buddy?

Open the Store from the top bar, browse skins, and purchase one with Doros. Then open your Inventory and click "Use" to equip it.

Can I reset the app tour?

Yes — click the button below to replay the interactive feature tour the next time you open the app.

What is AI Plan?

AI Plan breaks any goal into focused, actionable tasks automatically. Click "AI Plan" on the main screen, describe your goal, and let BuddyDoro build your to-do list.
